SAFETY GUIDE

Small checks can make a difference.

Use these practical steps when entering an unfamiliar room.

01

Inspect unusual objects

Pay attention to unfamiliar electronics, modified objects or devices that seem out of place.

02

Check private areas

Give extra attention to areas facing beds, bathrooms, changing spaces or work areas.

03

Investigate concerns

If something appears suspicious, avoid tampering and consider contacting the property or authorities.

04

Protect sensitive information

Keep passwords, financial details and private conversations away from unfamiliar environments.

!
IMPORTANT

Technology should support your awareness.

SafeScan is a front-end privacy-awareness interface. A browser application cannot reliably confirm that a hidden camera is present or absent. If you have a genuine safety concern, use appropriate professional inspection and report suspicious situations.
